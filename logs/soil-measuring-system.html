Soil Measuring System

Born from killing a few of my mother's plants by overwatering them while she was away. A soil moisture sensor and a temperature sensor feed an Arduino, which prints everything to an LCD so the guesswork disappears. Deliberately simple, and really a litmus test for a bigger project I have planned.

2023
ArduinoDHT11Analog SensingLCD

This project was less exciting, but was more of a litmus test for a bigger project I have planned (which I will execute later). Essentially, to rid myself of boredom, I realized a problem in my life, that I had been drinking too little water. My mother’s plants however, seemingly would die of too much water. I knew this because when my parents left on vacation, my mom gave me the duty of watering her plants and I would feed them like cows, heaps of abundance. Sadly, a few died in their greed and lack of self-control and I was held culprit. But in my defense, the dirt looked dry the next day anyways, how would I be able to tell that the plant was still full. I needed to develop a more accurate way for someone blind of sense like me.

I gathered a soil moisture sensor and a temperature sensor, the former would let me know if the soil was dry and the latter would let me know if the plant was sick (just kidding, it was just an accessory to fill up my LCD display).

Pairing everything up with my arduino, I dropped my code into my band of electronics and I got myself a display that told me everything I needed to know about my mom’s plants (very boring indeed).
