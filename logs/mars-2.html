Mars 2.0

A dual-mode surveillance rover built with my two friends Wahab and Sami, and one of my first extracurricular standouts. An Arduino drives two gear motors, with a Bluetooth manual mode through an app we built ourselves and an automatic mode that scans 180 degrees to avoid obstacles. It was the only robotics project in our grade that actually worked, and went on to win at school and place at the American Embassy exhibition.

2021
ArduinoC++CAD ModellingTinkercadBluetooth

0. on the launch pad?

I have to recall a lot of memories for this one, but I can acknowledge that as competitive brown kids, me and my friends' hangouts would usually be quite nerdy. This meant that every discussion was either tech or studies (gags). But this was one of my first ECA standouts, where me, and my two besties Wahab and Sami teamed up to leave a trail of ash in the mouths of other students in our batch, build a robot that no one has seen (meaning it's pretty bad).

1. lift off? or atleast trying to

We divided up alot of work, and honestly that was pretty nice since I had to do barely anything. Wahab stressed out till the night before our show and I just existed for emotional support! But to make myself look a bit more accomplished, let’s talk about the robot. It was driven by an Arduino, controlling two gear motors. The robot was pretty special, since it had the option of manual mode, which was bluetooth controlled with the help of a module and an app we built. The second state was automatic, meaning it could avoid obstacles by itself.

2. landing

The robot came off pretty well, as we explained its surveillance capabilities, being manually controlled as well as a camera to show the way. The automatic mode allowed it to navigate tight spaces by itself (since it was also compact) and could scan obstacles 180 degrees within its field of vision. The judges absolutely loved our project, as it was the only robotics project that worked in our grade! We also attracted external admiration, as an undercover judge from Boipori.com loved our robot. Considering our success locally in school, we participated at a bigger competition at the American Embassy and achieved distinction, even catching the eye of the American Ambassador. But what was more important, was that we had one up on everyone in our grade. Woohoo aura!
